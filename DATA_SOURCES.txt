Bundled privately for the user's cross-computer research handoff; not uploaded to Git.

PApple v4: https://zenodo.org/records/7260694
Local bundle uses original TRAIN 2304 RGB + paired visible/amodal VIA JSON,
and original VAL 814 RGB + paired visible/amodal VIA JSON.
The original validation byte/GT identity is stored in assets/papple_val_provenance.json.
Annotations are preserved, including visible/amodal geometric inconsistencies.
The parent checkpoint was trained on the PApple TRAIN split and selected on VAL.

MinneApple: https://github.com/nicolaihaeni/MinneApple
670 official train RGB + instance-ID PNG masks from the detection download.
Existing 570/100 train/validation filename assignment is preserved in the manifest.
Mask pixels, not converted YOLO boxes, are the supervision source.
The original manifest also references the 331 official test images, which are not bundled or used in this pilot.
MinneApple has visible instance masks; it does not provide the amodal labels required by this dual-branch model.
Unknown amodal supervision is omitted rather than assigned an empty mask.
